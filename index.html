🏁 Pit Window – Iron-man

The screen shows only the current stint time.
BLACK = do not pit yet · GREEN + WINDOW OPEN = pit allowed · FLASHING RED = window about to close (or closed) – pit NOW.
Previous stint times: top-left. Race time: top-right. Bottom-right: delta = current stint minus the average stint still needed (− early, + late). Past the max stint the screen goes plain black. Last stint: only the race time, big. The race screen always stays upright (portrait).

You touch the screen only at the race start and every time you leave the pits. Hold your finger anywhere on the screen until the bar at the bottom fills (prevents accidental touches). Draw 2 circles with your finger on the race screen to come back to this menu – the race keeps running, tap “Resume current race” to go back. The pit entry time is worked out as exit time − pit stop time.

Set Auto-Lock to Never.
